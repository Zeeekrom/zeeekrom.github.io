# Root Pages policy

- This public repository serves only the minimal LifeOps Atlas entry and
  hostname-root robots policy. Keep all personal and production data out.
- Project-original content is all rights reserved; preserve statutory
  exceptions, platform permissions and third-party licences.
- Keep the AI-crawler rules scoped to `/lifeops-atlas-showcase/`. Do not block
  other projects or ordinary search engines without the owner's instruction.
- Run `node --test test/policy.test.mjs` and review the diff before committing.
- A main-branch push deploys Pages. Obtain approval for future public changes;
  the initial publication was explicitly approved on 13 November 2026.
- Fetch before work, preserve local changes, and verify remote commits and
  live files after an approved deployment. Never automate LinkedIn access.
